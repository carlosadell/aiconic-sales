1. A lead books an intro call, now get them to show up

The booking is not the win. A call no one attends is a wasted slot. From the moment a lead books, reach out to them yourself and get a real reply that says they will be there. This is how we keep no shows down.

The goal is a confirmed yes, not a sent message. Sending one text and moving on is not the job. Reach out by every means you can, and keep going until the lead answers and tells you they will attend the call. A message they never replied to does not count. The system already sends the confirmation and reminders, but a real reply to a real person is what actually gets someone to show up.

The order to reach out in

  1. Personal SMS through the CRM, when there is a phone number. It usually reaches them fastest. Send it from the lead's card in Daily Outreach.
  2. No working phone? Do two things together. Email them from your own business inbox, not the CRM email, because your inbox always lands. And send a LinkedIn connection request with a short intro. Do not rely on LinkedIn alone, the request may sit unaccepted.

Make every message personal, and get a reply

  • Open the lead's card in Daily Outreach. Each channel has a ready draft. Edit it, never send it raw.
  • Before you write, read what you can: their survey answers, their LinkedIn profile, and the Conversify conversations. Name one specific thing about them or their business.
  • Match where they came from: LinkedIn leads, check their profile and Conversify first; email-campaign leads, try to find them on LinkedIn before you dial.
  • Keep it warm and first person, and sign it with your name.
  • Reach them on every channel you can, not just one, so the message lands somewhere.
  • Do not stop at one message. Keep following up until they reply and confirm they will be on the call. Ask them directly to confirm the time works.

3. Coordinate your own outreach with the automatic messages

The moment you move a lead into a stage, the CRM starts sending emails and SMS in your name. So before you send anything yourself, know which automatic messages have already gone out and check them, either on the lead's card in Daily Outreach or in the sequences above. If you skip this, the lead can end up getting messages from you that contradict what the system just sent, and that makes us look careless.

The point is to add to what they received, not repeat or clash with it. Do not copy the automatic message word for word, and do not fire a manual message on the same day one is already going out unless yours adds something new. When you add a LinkedIn note or a personal message, make it different and specific to them.

Each follow-up sequence runs on day 0, 1, 3, and 5, then stops, and after that it is on you. The moment a lead books, move them to the right stage so the reminders stop, otherwise they keep getting nudges after they have already booked.
