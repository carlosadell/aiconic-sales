Communications Playbook

Get booked leads to show up

This page shows you how to turn a booking into a lead who actually attends, and it shows you exactly what the CRM sends on its own so your messages work with it, never against it.

1

Get them to show up

The booking is not the win. A call nobody attends is a wasted slot. From the moment a lead books, you reach out personally and keep going until they reply and confirm they will be there.

The goal is a confirmed reply, not a sent message. Sending one text and moving on is not the job. Reach out by every means you can and keep going until the lead answers and tells you they will attend. A message they never replied to does not count. The system sends the confirmation and reminders on its own, but a real reply to a real person is what actually gets someone to show up.

The order to reach out in

  1. Personal SMS through the CRM first, when there is a phone number. It usually reaches them fastest. Send it from the lead card in Daily Outreach.
  2. No working phone? Do two things together. Email them from your own business inbox, not the CRM, because your inbox always lands. And send a LinkedIn connection request with a short intro. Do not rely on LinkedIn alone, the request can sit unaccepted.

Make every message personal and get a reply

  • Open the lead card in Daily Outreach. Each channel has a ready draft. Edit it, never send it raw.
  • Before you write, read their survey answers, their LinkedIn profile, and their Conversify conversations. Name one specific thing about them or their business.
  • Match where they came from, so the message fits how they found us.
  • Keep it warm and first person, and sign it with your name.
  • Reach them on every channel you can, not just one, so the message lands somewhere.
  • Do not stop at one message. Keep following up until they reply and confirm the time works. Ask them directly to confirm they will be on the call.

3

Coordinate your own outreach with the automatic messages

The moment you move a lead into a stage, the CRM starts sending emails and texts in your name. Your job is to add to what went out, never to contradict or repeat it.

Before you send anything yourself, check what already went out. You can see it on the lead card in Daily Outreach or in the sequences above. If you skip this, the lead can get a message from you that clashes with what the system just sent, and that makes us look careless.

Do not copy the automatic message word for word. Do not fire a manual message on the same day one is already going out unless yours adds something new. When you add a note or a personal message, make it different and specific to them.

Each sequence runs day 0, 1, 3 and 5, then stops, and after that it is on you. The moment a lead books, move them to the right stage so the reminders stop, otherwise they keep getting nudged after they have already booked.
