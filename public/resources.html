Everything about how we talk to a lead

One place for how we communicate with every lead: what you send before the call and how to keep it personal, what the system sends automatically, and how to keep it all coordinated so nothing clashes.

The golden rule. Every message the lead gets, automatic or from you, is first person from the assigned rep and signed [Your name] | Aiconic. To the lead it always reads as you, so keep your live conversations lined up with what they received.

1. Before the call, your personal touch

The system already sends the confirmation and reminders. On top of that, reach out personally before the call. A real message from a real person is what lifts the show-up rate, so this is not optional.

The order to reach out in

  1. Personal SMS through GoHighLevel, when there is a phone number. It usually reaches them fastest. Send it from the lead's card in Daily Outreach.
  2. No working phone? Do two things together. Email them from your own business inbox, not the GoHighLevel email, because your inbox always lands. And send a LinkedIn connection request with a short intro. Do not rely on LinkedIn alone, the request may sit unaccepted.

Make every message personal

  • Open the lead's card in Daily Outreach. Each channel has a ready draft. Edit it, never send it raw.
  • Before you write, read what you can: their survey answers, their LinkedIn profile, and the Conversify conversations. Name one specific thing about them or their business.
  • Match where they came from: LinkedIn leads, check their profile and Conversify first; email-campaign leads, try to find them on LinkedIn before you dial.
  • Keep it warm and first person, and sign it with your name.
  • Reach them on every channel you can, not just one, so the message lands somewhere.

3. How to coordinate your own outreach

Your personal messages and the automatic ones run at the same time. Keep them working together.

  1. Moving a stage sends messages in your name. The moment you move a lead into a stage, its sequence starts contacting them by email and SMS as you. Move stages promptly and to the correct one.
  2. Do not repeat the automation word for word. The lead is already getting your email and SMS. When you add a LinkedIn note or a personal message, make it different and specific to them.
  3. Send the same booking links. Use the intro or interview links below, never a different or personal link that could break or confuse them.
  4. Move the stage the moment they book. Booking removes them from the sequence, so leaving them in the stage keeps the reminders going after they have booked, which looks careless.
  5. Do not double up on the same day. Avoid firing a manual message on the same day an automated one goes out, unless yours adds something new.
  6. After day 5 it is on you. Each follow-up sequence runs day 0, 1, 3, and 5, then stops. Anything after that is manual.
